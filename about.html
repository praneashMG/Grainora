Our Journey

From Forest to Foundry

The evolution of a humble garage woodshop into a premier custom furniture studio.

Founded on a deep respect for natural materials, Grainora began with a simple mission: to rescue fallen trees from being chipped or burned and give them a second, permanent life. What started as a small operation crafting simple cutting boards and side tables quickly evolved as our passion for complex joinery and architectural design grew.

Today, we are a collective of master carpenters, resin artists, and metalworkers. We source our timber exclusively from sustainable urban logging and verified ethical mills. By combining ancient Japanese and European woodworking techniques with modern engineering, we create heirlooms designed to outlast the very trees they came from.

What Drives Us

Our Philosophy & Ethics

Craftsmanship is meaningless without respect for the environment and the materials we use.

Sustainable Sourcing

We exclusively use timber from salvaged urban trees, wind-fallen logs, and FSC-certified suppliers. No healthy forests are harmed for our craft.

Heirloom Quality

We reject the fast-furniture culture. Every piece is engineered with traditional joinery intended to last for generations, reducing landfill waste.

Non-Toxic Finishes

Our studio utilizes 0-VOC (Volatile Organic Compounds) plant-based hardwax oils that are completely safe for your home, children, and food contact.
