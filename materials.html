The Foundation

Premium Hardwoods

We hand-select each slab for its distinct grain pattern, structural integrity, and unique live edge character.

Black Walnut

Deep Rich Tones

Renowned for its deep chocolate colors and dramatic grain. The gold standard for luxury dining tables.

White Oak

Modern & Minimal

Incredibly dense and durable with a beautiful light, neutral tone. Perfect for contemporary spaces.

Parota

Exotic Contrast

Known for massive widths and striking contrast between its golden heartwood and creamy sapwood.

Curly Maple

Luminous Figure

A dense hardwood famous for its 3D "chatoyance" or curly figure that shimmers under lighting.

The Bond

Artisanal Resins

We use museum-grade, UV-resistant epoxy resins to fill voids, stabilize bark inclusions, or create striking "river" designs.

Obsidian Black

Opaque and matte, creating a stark, modern industrial contrast against warm wood.

Smoked Grey

Translucent tinted resin mimicking smoked glass for an elegant, understated aesthetic.

Crystal Clear

Glass-like transparency allowing you to see the natural raw edge suspended in time.

The Stance

Architectural Bases

A heavy slab requires a monumental foundation. We fabricate bases using robust, architectural-grade materials.

Powder-Coated Steel

Heavy-gauge welded steel, engineered to support massive weight without flexing, finished in matte black or custom geometric colors.

Brass & Copper

Solid brushed brass and aged copper accents for bowties, leg detailing, and custom inlays that add a touch of refined luxury.

Geometric Hardwood

Matching or contrasting hardwood bases crafted with traditional mortise and tenon joinery for a completely organic, cohesive look.

Protection

The Finishing Touch

We refuse to use plasticized polyurethanes. Our finishes enhance the wood's natural tactile feel while providing superior protection.

Ceramic Hardwax Oils

Plant-based oils infused with ceramic particles. They bond with the wood fibers at a molecular level, resisting water and wine without looking glossy.

Shou Sugi Ban

An ancient Japanese technique of charring the wood's surface. It creates a deep, textured charcoal finish that naturally repels moisture and pests.
