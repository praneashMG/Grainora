The Blueprint

Our Methodology

Four distinct phases ensure every piece meets our exacting standards of beauty, durability, and architectural precision.

1. Sourcing

Selecting the perfect raw slab from ethical, sustainable urban timber.

2. Design

Collaborative 3D mockups, resin color selection, and base engineering.

3. Crafting

Milling, epoxy pouring, metallic joinery, and meticulous flattening.

4. Delivery

Hand-sanding, ceramic oiling, and white-glove installation in your home.

The Foundation

Sourcing & Preparation

The integrity of a lasting piece begins years before the first cut is ever made.

01

Ethical Sourcing & Kiln Drying

We select only salvaged urban trees or wind-fallen logs. Before working with any slab, it spends up to two years air-drying, followed by months in a specialized vacuum kiln. This slow process reduces the moisture content to exactly 6-8%, ensuring the wood will never warp or crack in a climate-controlled home.

02

Milling & Surfacing

Once perfectly dried, the slab is mounted on our CNC router sled. We plane both sides entirely flat—a critical step many amateur woodworkers skip. We then carefully remove the loose bark by hand, using drawknives and wire brushes to preserve the natural organic shape of the live edge without damaging the cambium layer.

The Transformation

Engineering & Artistry

Where raw timber meets architectural precision and fluid resin.

The Epoxy Pour

We construct custom melamine molds to house the wood. Using deep-pour, UV-stable liquid epoxy, we carefully fill any voids, cracks, and rivers. This process often takes multiple pours and weeks to cure properly, ensuring a bubble-free, structurally sound bond that fuses the timber into a single monolith.

Metallic Joinery

We don't just rely on glue. For natural checks (cracks) that we wish to leave open without resin, we hand-route and inlay solid brass or copper bowties. These act as mechanical stitches across the grain, preventing the crack from ever spreading while adding a distinct, luxurious architectural detail.

The Final Polish

Refinement & Delivery

The meticulous final touches that transform a massive slab into a flawless, tactile masterpiece.

Silken Sanding & Ceramic Oils

The true mark of luxury furniture is how it feels. We spend hours progressively sanding the piece up to 800-grit until it feels as smooth as glass. We then apply two coats of a proprietary ceramic hardwax oil. It bonds at a molecular level, offering immense durability against spills while keeping the authentic matte feel of real wood.

White-Glove Installation

These are not flat-pack tables. A completed 10-foot Walnut table can easily weigh over 400 pounds. Our team personally delivers and installs the heavy steel bases, precisely centering and securing the top in your home or boardroom to ensure absolute perfection.
